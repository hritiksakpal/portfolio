Case study / 02

Making FreeSplit easy to enter, understand, share and settle.

FreeSplit turns group expenses into a clear balance. People can add bills, see who owes whom, share the result directly on WhatsApp, and open the shared ledger without logging in.

01 / Problem

Group expenses get messy when many people pay.

A trip can have different bills, different payers, and different people sharing each bill. The product needs to keep those details simple and turn them into one clear result.

The product needs to answer four simple questions.

Who is in the group? Who paid for this bill? Who should share the bill? After all bills are added, who owes whom?

FreeSplit keeps these questions close to the point where the user makes each decision instead of asking them to remember the whole calculation.

Who is in the group? Who paid? Who shares? Who owes whom?
Product goal
Clear answer, fewer calculations
02 / End-to-end flow

One flow from the first bill to the final settlement.

The product is designed around the real job to be done: record the money, understand the result, tell the group, and close the balance.

01

Start the split

Create an event, choose the currency, and add people by name. A phone number is optional.

02

Add each bill

Select who paid, add a simple description, enter the amount, and choose who shares that bill.

03

Check the ledger

Keep every bill visible so users can see what was paid and who was included.

04

See the balance

Combine the bills into a clear “who owes whom” result instead of making users do the math themselves.

05

Share and settle

Share the summary on WhatsApp, open the ledger from the link, pay by UPI when available, or mark the balance as settled.

No login for the recipient. The person who receives the ledger link can open the shared event and view the ledger without creating an account. This keeps sharing simple when the other person only needs to check the result.
04 / Product choices

Why these choices matter.

FreeSplit is built around the moment when people need an answer, not around a long account setup. That is why the flow keeps people lightweight, makes the ledger easy to open, and puts sharing close to the final balance.

Add by name Phone optional Share on WhatsApp No-login ledger
