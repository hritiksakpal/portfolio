Case study / 03

Turning a messy study-resource problem into a clear discovery flow.

MBA Solutions Hub is an academic support product built around a practical question: how can students find the right paper and solution with less searching and less confusion?

Back to portfolio
Product typeEducation / content platform
Primary userMBA students preparing for exams
Core problemFragmented academic resources
FocusIA, UX, discovery and monetisation
01 / Problem

The content can be useful and still be hard to find.

The product starts with a familiar student workflow: identify the semester, find the subject, locate the question paper and then decide whether the available solution is useful.

User pain points

Students often search across multiple pages, chats or folders for academic resources. The product opportunity is to make discovery predictable by giving content a consistent hierarchy.

02 / Information architecture

One predictable hierarchy.

The core experience is deliberately simple so the user does not need to understand how the platform is organised before finding what they came for.

01

Semester

Start with the broad academic context so the result set is immediately relevant.

02

Subject

Reduce the content set to the subject the student is preparing for.

03

Question paper

Make the actual paper easy to identify before asking the user to take another action.

04

Solution

Keep the solution attached to the relevant paper rather than making the student search for it separately.

03 / Business model

Free discovery, paid value.

The monetisation model was designed around a simple distinction: let students discover and evaluate content before asking them to pay for higher-value access.

Free layer

Question-paper discovery remains accessible so the product can serve the top of the funnel and build trust around the content structure.

DiscoveryNavigationPreview

Value layer

Solutions and downloads can be positioned as higher-value actions, supported by a clear monetisation path rather than interrupting the initial discovery flow.

Rewarded accessPaid downloadsBundles

“For content products, information architecture is part of the product value. Finding the right resource is the first successful outcome.”

What I would measure next

Measure discovery before monetisation.

The priority metrics would be search/discovery success, paper-view rate, solution-intent rate, paid conversion, download completion and repeat study sessions.
