Case study / 03

Making previous-year solved papers easy to find and study.

MBA Solutions Hub is an academic study product built to help MBA students find previous-year question papers, open solved answers, and use them as a simple reference for exam preparation.

01 / Product screens

How students use the product.

The screens show the main study flow: find a previous-year paper, open the questions, and reach the solved answers.

Product goalHelp students find previous-year solved papers they can refer to while preparing for exams. The access step supports the business model; it is not the main reason for the product.
02 / Problem

Students need past papers to study, but finding them can take time.

The product starts with a simple study task: choose the semester, find the subject, locate a previous-year question paper, and use the solved answer as a reference.

User pain points

Students can spend time looking through different pages or saved files just to find the paper they need. The product makes that first step easier by keeping papers in one clear structure.

03 / Study flow

A simple path from semester to solution.

The core experience keeps the path easy to understand: start broad, narrow the subject, choose the paper, then open the solved answer.

04 / Business model

Support the product, not the goal.

The business layer comes after the main study task. Students should be able to find the right paper first, then choose how they want to access higher-value solutions or downloads.

Free layer

Students can discover and compare previous-year question papers before taking any paid action.

Value layer

Solutions and downloads can be offered through clear access options after the student has found the paper they need.

What I would measure next

Measure discovery before monetisation.

The priority metrics would be paper discovery success, paper-view rate, solution access rate, download completion, paid conversion and repeat study sessions.
