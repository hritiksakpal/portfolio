HOW I THINK

Clarity before complexity.

My default product loop is deliberately simple: understand the user, define the logic, ship a useful version, then improve it.

01

Discover

Understand the user, business context, constraints and the job the product needs to do.

02

Define

Translate ambiguity into requirements, flows, product logic, priorities and clear acceptance criteria.

03

Build

Work closely with design and development, while staying hands-on enough to understand the implementation.

04

Improve

Use feedback, analytics and observed friction to decide what should change next.
